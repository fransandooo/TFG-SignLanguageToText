\section{AI-Based Systems Ethical Impact}
An ethical analysis in Artificial Intelligence is a process which consist in examining the implications, preoccupations and challenges associated with the development and
deployment of these systems. With the recent advancements, AI is starting to integrate in applications which can be considered critical to society, that's why their technical improvement
and evaluation should also be accompanied by an ethics review. This ethical impact review on AI systems is based on 4 general pillars:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Human Rights and Fairness: it is necessary to prevent biases and discrimination against minority groups to ensure a fairness in treatment.
    \item Accountability: it is crucial to establish who is responsible for the decisions the AI systems make, this could either be the development team, the users, the company, or even the system itself
    \item Privacy and Security: it must be ensured that the user data is protected and that the system doesn't infringe any confidentiality of the users.
    \item Transparency: it is indispensable to provide clarity on how the system is taking the decision and what are they based on, meaning black boxes system should be avoided.
\end{itemize}
\section{Ethical Dimensions Descriptions}
Now that we have defined why an ethics review must be done and what pillars it is based on. To standardize this ethics reviews, many organizations have developed different frameworks such as The European Ethics guidelines for trustworthy AI (CITA EU), and The Principles of AI by Microsoft. In this case to evaluate the system, we are going to adopt the Google's AI Principles, this framework covers seven fundamental dimensions which guide the responsible development:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Be socially beneficial:} The AI system needs to aim at solving a real societal problem and actually do it effectively.
    \item \textbf{Avoid creating or reinforcing unfair bias:} We need to take steps to ensure that the AI system is fair and inclusive for everyone, avoiding discrimination.
    \item \textbf{Be built and tested for safety:} Safety has to be a priority in how the system is designed and developed.
    \item \textbf{Be accountable to people:} The system must provide transparency and let users have some control over it.
    \item \textbf{Incorporate privacy design principles:} The AI has to be designed in a way that protects user data and privacy.
    \item \textbf{Uphold high standards of scientific excellence:} The development of the AI must be based on rigorous research and sound scientific principles.
    \item \textbf{Be made available for uses that accord with these principles:} We need to be clear about the intended use cases for the AI, making sure it is used properly and aligns with these values.
\end{enumerate}
